## The 90-Second Demo Narrative

### **0:00–0:15 — The Problem**
**[Visual On Screen]**  
*The screen opens on a smartphone frame displaying the VitaChain app in "Offline Mode" (indicated by a slashed WiFi icon). The camera cuts between the UI and a short clip/photo of a rural health setting. Amara is typing into the VitaChain Health Companion.*

**[Narrator / Pitcher]**  
"Meet Amara. Her mother just developed a severe fever, but they live hours away from a clinic, and the internet here is completely out. In the Global South, the 'baton drop' between isolated patients and fragmented clinics costs hundreds of thousands of lives. Let's fix that."

---

### **0:15–0:40 — Offline AI & Universal Health Passport**
**[Visual On Screen]**  
*Amara's screen: She asks, "My mother has a 39°C fever and severe joint pain." The app processes using TinyLlama 1.1B (an offline indicator flashes). It provides triage advice for Malaria/Dengue. Amara taps 'Generate Passport'. A W3C Verifiable Credential QR Code instantly renders on her screen. The scene switches to a Clinician scanning the QR code with their own device, instantly importing Amara's mother's offline clinical summary.*

**[Narrator / Pitcher]**  
"With VitaChain, Amara consults her offline AI guardian. Our on-device model provides instant triage. When Amara finally reaches a clinic, she generates an offline Universal Health Passport via QR code. The clinician scans it—no internet required—instantly receiving an encrypted, structured clinical summary."

---

### **0:40–1:10 — Mesh Outbreak Alert & Admin Oversight**
**[Visual On Screen]**  
*The Clinician's dashboard logs the suspected Malaria case. The screen splits, showing three nearby offline phones triggering a silent Bluetooth Mesh gossip protocol. A 'Community Health Worker (CHW)' dashboard suddenly flashes red: "Outbreak Alert: High fever cluster detected." The screen transitions to the Admin Dashboard showing analytics mapping the hotspot.*

**[Narrator / Pitcher]**  
"But health doesn't exist in a vacuum. As the clinician logs the diagnosis, VitaChain's peer-to-peer Bluetooth mesh gossips this anonymized signal to nearby devices. A localized outbreak alert is automatically generated on the Community Health Worker's dashboard, triggering rapid intervention before the outbreak spreads, all supervised securely from the Admin Analytics hub."

---

### **1:10–1:30 — Full Circle & Call to Action**
**[Visual On Screen]**  
*The smartphone returns to Amara's view. Her Personal Health Graph updates beautifully with a new 'Encounter' and 'Medication' block. The screen transitions to the VitaChain logo, tagging along the bottom: 'Powered by Gemma 4, TinyLlama, and Squad'.*

**[Narrator / Pitcher]**  
"The referral loop is closed, and Amara's self-sovereign Health Graph is updated. VitaChain is an offline-first AI guardian, a mesh intelligence network, and a decentralized health infrastructure—all running in your pocket. Thank you."

## Phase 3 Zero-Cost Features (Optional Demo Segments)

- **Lab Report OCR**: Upload an image of a lab report; VitaChain extracts text using Tesseract.js and generates a plain-language explanation with color-coded status.
- **Guest Mode PIN**: Set a PIN to protect health data on shared devices; requires PIN entry for each session.
- **Voice-Only Mode**: Toggle voice interaction; use Whisper-tiny for speech-to-text and TTS for AI responses, enabling hands-free usage.
- **Simplified CrisisPopup**: One-touch emergency call button directly from any screen; instantly dials local emergency services.
